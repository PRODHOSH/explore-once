# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this to be a friendly, respectful place to learn and build, no matter your background or experience level.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Using the project to target, scrape or harm other people, or to get around the rules of the sites the skills touch.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. If you see a problem, report it privately to the maintainer through the contact links on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled with care and kept confidential.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, discussions and pull requests.

This code is adapted from the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
